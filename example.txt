"You are welcome, most noble Sorceress, to the land of the Munchkins.
We are so grateful to you for having killed the Wicked Witch of the East,
and for setting our people free from bondage.
"Dorothy listened to this speech with wonder. What could the little woman
possibly mean by calling her a sorceress, and saying she had killed
the Wicked Witch of the East? Dorothy was an innocent,
harmless little girl, who had been carried by a cyclone many miles from home;
and she had never killed anyone in all her life.But the little woman went on saying,
"The Wicked Witch of the East, as I said, is a prisoner no longer; she is dead, and
here are her shoes, which are now yours." And she pointed to the two feet of the
dead woman, which stuck out from beneath the heavy beam of the house.
